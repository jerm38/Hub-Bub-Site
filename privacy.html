Hub-bub Private Policy

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

1. About Hub-Bub

The Hub-bub is a privately operated family dashboard and home automation project. It currently provides household organization elements such as a grocery list and chores/To-Do lists. Additionally, it displays the family schedule by integrating Google Calendar API.

2. Information Accessed

The Hub-bub request read-only access to authorized Google Calendar information, which includes calendar events dates and times as well as the event descriptions.

3) How Information Is Used

The information recieved from Google Calendar is used to dispaly a family monthly schedule and upcoming events on the Hub-bub dashboard.

4. Data Storage and Security

The Hub-bub operates on a privately owned home server and Google OAuth credentials are used to authorize access to Google Calendar.

The information received from Google Calendar is intended for display within the family dashboard and is NOT published through this website.

5. Information Sharing

The Hub-Bub does NOT sell Google Calendar Information or use it for advertising. Calendar information is displayed to the authorized household users only.

6. Revoking Access

Users can revoke Hub-Bub's access to their Google Account through their security settings
